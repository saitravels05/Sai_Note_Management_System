## 1. Roadmap Policy & Governance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No feature listed below may be built during V1.0.**  
> V1.0 must be accepted, deployed, and proven in daily production operations for at least 30 days before initiating V1.1 engineering.

Features are evaluated based on: **Business Value**, **Operational Safety**, **Security Impact**, and **Accounting Integrity**.

---

## 2. Candidate Enhancements for V1.1

### Feature 1: OCR Receipt & Fuel Bill Scanning
- **Concept:** Allow drivers or office clerks to photograph diesel slips or toll receipts on their mobile phones.
- **Workflow:** AI vision model extracts Date, Pump Name, Gallons/Liters, and Total Amount, generating a pre-filled **Expense Draft**.
- **Accounting Safety:** Draft remains unposted until an Accountant reviews and confirms the entry.

### Feature 2: Automated WhatsApp Notifications & Reminders
- **Concept:** Send booking confirmations, payment receipts, and balance reminders to passengers via the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
- **Security Guard:** Only transactional messages (opt-in); passenger phone numbers remain encrypted and protected under tenant isolation.

### Feature 3: Automated Bank Statement Reconciliation
- **Concept:** Import `.csv` / `.xlsx` statements directly downloaded from SBI, HDFC, or ICICI Bank net banking.
- **Engine Rule:** Automatically matches incoming UPI/NEFT transactions against outstanding customer receivables and suggests 1-click allocations.

### Feature 4: GST Export Enhancements (GSTR-1 JSON)
- **Concept:** Generate GSTR-1 offline JSON files directly from confirmed month-end closing packs, enabling 1-click upload to the government GST portal.

### Feature 5: Distributed Redis Cache & Horizontal Scaling
- **Concept:** Upgrade in-memory rate limiting and session validation to use an external managed Redis instance, enabling zero-downtime horizontal auto-scaling across multiple cloud nodes.

### Feature 6: Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline Capabilities
- **Concept:** Enable offline tour notes and passenger list caching on mobile devices for drivers operating in remote areas with intermittent cellular coverage.

## 3. Dedicated Travel Booking Integration Strategy

If **Sai Tours & Travels** later decides to integrate live airline GDS (Amadeus/Galileo) or IRCTC rail booking engines:
- It will be treated as an independent architectural module (`TravelBookingService`).
- Accounting entries will flow into the existing Phase 5 double-entry engine via standard `INCOME` and `RECEIVABLE` journal adapters, preserving core financial isolation.
